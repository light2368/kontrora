Full platform

Everything your agency runs on

Nine modules, one product—the full picture lives here; deep dives and flows are on the Features page.

Intelligent Capacity Planning

Proportional allocation across accounts. Know exactly who's available before committing to new work - at individual, department, and org level.

Workflow automation

Node-based visual editor with role-based handoffs, approvals, and form steps—so SOPs become living processes instead of static docs.

Time tracking

Clock in/out widget, manual entries, weekly summaries, auto clock-out guardrails, and edit windows—so billable time stays accurate.

Projects & tasks

Kanban and table views, assignments, milestones, issues, and updates—plus Gantt, table, and workflow layouts when you need them.

RBAC & permissions

About 40 granular permissions, dynamic roles, and superadmin controls—with a fast path from loaded role data and a database fallback. Rules are enforced with Supabase Row Level Security, not UI-only checks.

Client portal

A dedicated client experience so accounts can view work, leave feedback, and approve deliverables—without giving clients your full internal tool.

Analytics

Org, department, account, project, and time metrics with Recharts and D3—so leaders see utilization, delivery health, and trends in one place.

Accounts & departments

CRM-style account records and a department hierarchy so capacity, work, and reporting roll up the way your firm is actually structured.

Invitations & newsletters

Email-based onboarding for staff and clients (SMTP), plus internal newsletter broadcasting so updates reach the whole org without another tool.
