Features

Built for how agencies actually work

Every feature in Worklo was designed to solve a real operational problem.

What it is

Internal ops, productized

One product for how agencies and consultancies actually run - projects, time, capacity, clients, and reporting in one place.

Capacity Planning

Know before you commit

Real-time visibility into every team member availability before you say yes to new work.

  • Proportional allocation across accounts
  • Individual, department, and org-level utilization views
  • Historical capacity trends and forecasting
  • Availability calendar with leave and holiday support

Workflow Automation

SOPs that actually get followed

Transform your SOPs into visual, enforced workflows with guardrails that guide your team.

  • Drag-and-drop workflow builder with node-based editor
  • Department handoffs, role assignments, client approvals
  • Complete audit trail for every transition
  • Automated notifications on handoffs and blockers
Time Tracking

Flexible, accurate, effortless

Clock in when you start, allocate hours across tasks when done. Or log manually.

  • Clock in/out with task allocation on clock-out
  • Manual entry for retroactive logging
  • Auto clock-out after 16 hours
  • 14-day edit window for corrections
  • Admin view with full team time analytics

Every view your team needs

Switch between Kanban, Gantt, Table, and Workflow views instantly.

  • Workflow views through custom approval stages

Security & Permissions

Security at every layer

~40 permissions across 15 categories, enforced at the database level with PostgreSQL RLS.

  • Role-based access control with custom role creation
  • Context-aware permissions based on project assignments
  • Row Level Security on every database table
  • Audit logging for all critical changes
  • Rate limiting and input validation
