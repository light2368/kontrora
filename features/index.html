Internal ops, productized

One product for how agencies and consultancies actually run - projects, time, capacity, clients, and reporting in one place.

  • Historical capacity trends and forecasting

Workflow Automation

SOPs that actually get followed

Transform your SOPs into visual, enforced workflows with guardrails that guide your team.

  • Drag-and-drop workflow builder with node-based editor
  • Department handoffs, role assignments, client approvals
  • Complete audit trail for every transition
  • Automated notifications on handoffs and blockers
Time Tracking

Flexible, accurate, effortless

Clock in when you start, allocate hours across tasks when done. Or log manually.

  • Clock in/out with task allocation on clock-out
  • Manual entry for retroactive logging
  • Auto clock-out after 16 hours
  • 14-day edit window for corrections
  • Admin view with full team time analytics
